# Encode Team Introduction Script

## Format

- Length: 45-60 seconds.
- Separate from the 3-minute product demo.
- Use the real names, roles, and backgrounds. Replace every bracketed field before recording.
- A phone or webcam recording is sufficient. Clear audio matters more than production effects.

## Script

> Hi, we are the OpenRails team. I am [name], [role], and my background is [one sentence about engineering, product, payments, infrastructure, or the relevant experience]. I am building OpenRails because work increasingly happens across people, businesses, applications, and agents, but the payment layer often loses the context that makes a payment accountable.
>
> OpenRails is our attempt to make delegated work programmable and inspectable. It connects operating context, bounded authority, accepted terms, proof of work, and USDC settlement without taking custody of the owner's wallet.
>
> We want to take part in the Accelerator to harden the product on Arc, validate the agent-facing integration with real builders, and turn the current MVP into infrastructure that businesses, creators, merchants, and agents can use in production. We are especially interested in feedback on the authorization model, the settlement experience, and the path from a working testnet demonstration to real adoption.
>
> We are [solo / a team of ...]. Our immediate focus is [one concrete next milestone]. Thank you for considering OpenRails.

## Team Version

If more than one person appears, each person should state their name and role in one sentence. Keep the shared explanation and accelerator motivation as one speaker-led close so the video stays under one minute.

## Do Not Claim

- Do not describe interested developers or businesses as production customers.
- Do not list integrations that are not visible in the current product or documented as boundaries.
- Do not read the product demo script in this video. The team video is about who is building OpenRails and why the Accelerator is the right next step.
